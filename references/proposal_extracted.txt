                                             Abstract

         Predicting international economic relationships is vital for trade risk management
         and macro-financial stability. While Temporal Knowledge Graphs (TKGs) model
         structured event sequences, standard graph methods struggle with multi-scale
         temporal dynamics. We propose a hybrid predictive framework decoupling event
         extraction from numerical sequence forecasting. Using the GDELT database,
         we isolate country-level economic cooperation events, extract dynamic graph
         features, and feed these numerical series into Timestral, a time-series foundation
         model. We map Timestral’s forecasts into edge probabilities over a 30-day horizon
         using a calibrated logistic function. We evaluate link prediction accuracy against
         topological and statistical baselines in a walk-forward backtest.


1   Introduction & Motivation
International economic networks evolve under geopolitical and market forces, propagating shifts into
trade flows and financial markets. Temporal Knowledge Graph Completion (TKGC) aims to predict
missing or future temporal links (ei , r, ej , t + ∆t). However, existing TKG models rely on static
embeddings or simple recurrent architectures that fail to capture non-stationary dynamics.
Pretrained foundation models like Timestral (3) excel at zero-shot time-series forecasting but operate
strictly on numerical sequences. To bridge this gap, we present a two-stage pipeline: (1) We
construct a TKG of country-level economic cooperation using the structured GDELT dataset (1).
(2) We compute historical interaction counts and topological features for entity pairs and pass these
numerical series into Timestral to forecast future interaction intensity. Mapping this forecast to edge
formation probability over a 30-day horizon (H = 30) provides an interpretable framework for
dynamic link forecasting.

3.3   Baselines & Evaluation Protocol
We perform a strict walk-forward backtest with non-overlapping 30-day evaluation windows. We
benchmark against: (1) Static Topological Metrics (Jaccard Similarity, Common Neighbors at T );
(2) Classical Time Series (ARIMA (9), Prophet (10)); and (3) Static Logistic Regression on rolling
topological features. Models are evaluated using **ROC-AUC**, **Precision@K**, and **MRR**.
